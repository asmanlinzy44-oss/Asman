Authentic Past Papers, FWC Pilot Examinations, School Term Tests, and Complete Marking Schemes for Physical Science (Combined Maths) & Biological Science (Bio) Students.

Comprehensive Examination Repository & Academic Study Hub

Paper Express is dedicated to empowering Sri Lankan Advanced Level students with organized, verified, and completely free study materials. Our platform provides direct Google Drive access to official question papers and marking schemes from 1981 to present, supporting students in maximizing their exam performance and Z-score ranking.

Supported A/L Science Streams & Core Disciplines

  • Physical Science (Combined Mathematics Stream): Pure Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Classical Mechanics, Physics, and Chemistry.
  • Biological Science (Biology Stream): Cell Biology, Plant and Animal Physiology, Genetics, Molecular Biology, Environmental Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.

Available Academic Portals & Study Folders

  • National G.C.E. A/L Past Papers (1981–2024): Authentic past papers with structured essay solutions and official grading criteria issued by the Department of Examinations, Sri Lanka.
  • FWC Pilot & School Term Test Papers: Renowned FWC Thondaimanaru pilot exams alongside 1st, 2nd, and 3rd term evaluation papers organized by term and subject.
  • Academic Resources & Master Formula Sheets: Unit-by-unit study guides, formula summaries, and Google Drive resource folders.
  • University of Moratuwa Pilot Assessments: Prestigious university-level pilot examinations and step-by-step marking schemes.
